## About me

I'm an **Electrical Engineering PhD researcher at the University of Missouri** developing compact scientific sensing instruments. My work spans **MEMS biosensor fabrication, optical and electronic sensing, SolidWorks/Fusion 360 design, 3D-printed prototypes, embedded controllers, Python/Django instrumentation, and experimental data analysis**.

I enjoy engineering the complete system—from a fabricated sensing device and its mechanical assembly to automated acquisition, signal processing, and experimental evaluation.

## Featured engineering projects

### [Compact Impedance Sensing System Platform](https://github.com/Kamran00786/Compact-Impedance-Sensing-System-Platform)

**MEMS biosensors · SolidWorks/Fusion 360 · 3D printing · Raspberry Pi 5/4 · UART · Django**

An integrated impedance-measurement instrument featuring custom sensor fixtures, embedded hardware control, calibration and acquisition workflows, and multi-electrode experiment analysis and reporting.

### [Compact Fiber-Optic SERS Platform](https://github.com/Kamran00786/Compact-Fiber-Optic-SERS-Sensing-Platform)

**Fiber-optic SERS · Optical instrumentation · 3D CAD · Raman spectroscopy · Python/Django**

A compact spectroscopic sensing platform combining optical and mechanical integration, spectrometer/laser control, automated Raman acquisition, **spectral data processing and analysis**, visualization, and reporting.

## Engineering expertise

- **Mechanical design & prototyping:** SolidWorks, Autodesk Fusion 360, instrument enclosures, fixtures, 3D CAD, 3D printing, system assembly.
- **Embedded systems & software:** Raspberry Pi 4/5, ESP32, Arduino, UART, Python, Django, FastAPI, C++, hardware–software integration.
