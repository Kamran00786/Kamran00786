## About me

I'm an **Electrical Engineering PhD researcher at the Microsystems Research Lab, University of Missouri**, specializing in **MEMS biosensors, semiconductor microfabrication, and cleanroom process development**. My hands-on experience spans **L-Edit mask design, photolithography, Cr/Au thin-film deposition, RF/DC sputtering, wet and dry etching, electroplating, and PDMS packaging** of sensing devices on glass and silicon substrates.

I enjoy developing the **complete instrument—from sensor fabrication and device characterization to fully integrated sensing systems**. My work bridges semiconductor processing with practical scientific instrumentation: I use **microscopy, profilometry, and electrical characterization** to evaluate fabricated devices, then integrate them into compact platforms through **SolidWorks/Fusion 360 mechanical design, custom 3D-printed components, embedded hardware, and Python/Django-based instrument control, data acquisition, signal processing, and analysis**.

## Featured engineering projects

### [Compact Impedance Sensing System Platform](https://github.com/Kamran00786/Compact-Impedance-Sensing-System-Platform)

**MEMS biosensors · SolidWorks/Fusion 360 · 3D printing · Raspberry Pi 5/4 · UART · Django**

An integrated impedance-measurement instrument featuring custom sensor fixtures, embedded hardware control, calibration and acquisition workflows, and multi-electrode experiment analysis and reporting.

### [Compact Fiber-Optic SERS Platform](https://github.com/Kamran00786/Compact-Fiber-Optic-SERS-Sensing-Platform)

**Fiber-optic SERS · Optical instrumentation · 3D CAD · Raman spectroscopy · Python/Django**

A compact spectroscopic sensing platform combining optical and mechanical integration, spectrometer/laser control, automated Raman acquisition, **spectral data processing and analysis**, visualization, and reporting.

## Engineering expertise

- **MEMS fabrication & semiconductor processes:** L-Edit mask design, mask alignment, photolithography, Cr/Au thin films, RF/DC sputtering and PVD, wet/dry etching, electroplating, PDMS packaging, wafer/device processing.
- **Semiconductor equipment & metrology:** Experience with CVD/PECVD/ALD and RIE/DRIE equipment; SEM, optical microscopy, step profilometry, ellipsometry, four-point probe testing, and device inspection.
- **Biosensors & instrumentation:** MEMS impedance biosensors, fiber-optic SERS, Raman spectroscopy, sensor testing, experimental acquisition and analysis.
- **Mechanical design & prototyping:** SolidWorks, Autodesk Fusion 360, instrument enclosures and fixtures, 3D CAD, 3D printing, system assembly.
- **Embedded systems & scientific software:** Raspberry Pi 4/5, ESP32, Arduino, UART, Python, Django, FastAPI, C++, data acquisition, spectral signal processing, instrument automation.
