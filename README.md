## About me

I'm an **Electrical Engineering PhD researcher at the Microsystems Research Lab, University of Missouri**, specializing in **MEMS biosensors, semiconductor microfabrication, and cleanroom process development**. My hands-on experience spans **L-Edit mask design, photolithography, Cr/Au thin-film deposition, RF/DC sputtering, wet and dry etching, electroplating, and PDMS packaging** of sensing devices on glass and silicon substrates.

I enjoy developing the **complete instrument—from sensor fabrication and device characterization to fully integrated sensing systems**. My work bridges semiconductor processing with practical scientific instrumentation: I use **microscopy, profilometry, and electrical characterization** to evaluate fabricated devices, then integrate them into compact platforms through **SolidWorks/Fusion 360 mechanical design, custom 3D-printed components, embedded hardware, and Python/Django-based instrument control, data acquisition, signal processing, and analysis**.

**Fiber-optic SERS Fabrication · Optical instrumentation ·  SolidWorks/Fusion 360 3D Design · Raman spectroscopy · Python/Django Web Interface**

A compact spectroscopic sensing platform combining optical and mechanical integration, spectrometer/laser control, automated Raman acquisition, **spectral data processing and analysis**, visualization, and reporting.
